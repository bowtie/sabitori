My expensive mouse kept reversing while scrolling and jumping randomly. Turns out the
[Razer DeathAdder V3 Pro scroll wheel](https://www.reddit.com/r/MouseReview/search/?q=deathadder+v3+pro+scroll+wheel)
has a known issue, and rather than resort to soldering, I decided to solve it in
software.

Sabitori adds three modes you can toggle from the settings window:

- **Scroll Lock** disables the wheel entirely. Good for mice that jump on
  their own, or when you have jump bound to scroll and don't want it firing
  while you're clicking.
- **Direction Lock** blocks the opposite direction for a configurable timeout
  after each scroll, so the page goes where you intended instead of jittering
  up and down.
- **Pass Through** does nothing. The wheel works as-is.
